---

### How to Enable GitHub Pages (if not already enabled)
1. In your GitHub repository: Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Pages** (under the "Code and automation" section).
2. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. Within a minute or two, your Privacy Policy will be live at:
   `https://ahammadalburooj.github.io/privacypolicyformunajat/`
5. Paste this URL into your **Google Play Console** under **App content &rarr; Privacy policy**.
