Privacy Policy

Burooj 4 Tech ("we," "our," or "us") built the Munajat mobile application (the "App") as a digital Islamic Adhkar and supplication companion. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our App.

By downloading and using Munajat, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

No Account Required

Use all features without sign-up, registration, or login credentials.

Stored On-Device

Preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress stay safely on your phone.

No Data Selling

We do not collect, monetize, sell, or rent your personal information.

1 Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We respect your privacy and designed Munajat to work without user registration or personal accounts.

Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Local Preferences & App State

Your custom app settings—such as font zoom levels, reading progress, bookmark selections, audio playback states, and language preferences—are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.

Share Feature & Temporary Media Cache

When you tap the "Share" button to share an Adhkar card or text with others, the App temporarily generates an image or text snippet on your device solely to pass it to your chosen sharing app (such as WhatsApp or Instagram). No files are uploaded to our servers.

Audio Playback

Audio recitations bundled with or streamed through the App are used strictly for playback functionality and do not record or monitor your microphone.

Location Data (Optional)

If you enable features requiring location, the App accesses your approximate or precise location (COARSE_LOCATION / FINE_LOCATION) strictly on your device to calculate accurate timings or directions. Location data is never stored or shared externally.

2 Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App may use standard Android platform services provided by Google to ensure smooth delivery and stability:

Google Play Services

Used for distributing app updates and collecting anonymized crash logs/diagnostics (such as device model and Android OS version) to help us identify and fix technical bugs.

No Advertising or Data Selling

Munajat does not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

3 Data Safety, Retention, and Deletion

No Cloud Storage

Because we do not collect personal user data on external servers, there is no remote database storing your personal activity.

User Control & Deletion

All locally saved preferences remain on your phone until you delete them. You can permanently erase all App data at any time by going to your Android device's Settings → Apps → Munajat → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

4 Children’s Privacy

Munajat is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we add new features to Munajat or to comply with Google Play policy updates. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
